Comparing two career directions

Forward deployed engineering

Evidence: Catena emphasizes customer onboarding, troubleshooting, integration pipelines and translating field feedback into product work.

Portfolio proof: API integration, bug investigation and clear user-facing documentation.

Tradeoff: frequent customer contact and ambiguous operational problems.

AI application engineering

Evidence: Makai emphasizes AI features, LLM workflows and evaluation; Arizent adds domain-specific data pipelines and reliability.

Portfolio proof: a small AI feature with repeatable evaluations, error handling and tests.

Tradeoff: rapidly changing tools and additional data/LLM evaluation skills.

Provisional choice: AI application engineering, while retaining customer-facing delivery as a second direction. Next step: implement and test a bounded Python feature with an API, log failure cases and write a concise case study. This is a research direction, not a claim of job readiness.
